Why Is My Website Traffic Dropping When My Rankings Haven't Changed?

Priya Jha · November 2026 · 7 min read

A founder showed me their dashboard. Rankings looked the same, but traffic was down by a third. The SEO report said nothing was wrong, and something clearly was.

Why is my traffic dropping if my rankings haven't changed?

People still search, they just click less. Google now puts an AI-written answer at the top of many results, and a lot of people read it and stop there.

Pew Research tracked the browsing of 900 US adults in March 2025. When Google showed an AI summary, they clicked a regular result in 8% of visits. When it didn't, they clicked in 15% of visits. Ahrefs compared 300,000 keywords between December 2023 and December 2025 and found that the top-ranking page had a click-through rate about 58% lower when an AI Overview appeared.

Then there's everything outside Google. Shoppers now ask ChatGPT or Perplexity which serum to buy or which supplier to call. If your brand isn't named in that answer, you never get to compete on page one.

So your ranking can stay exactly where it was while your traffic falls. To be honest, that's the part most SEO reports miss.

How do I know if AI answers are taking my clicks?

  1. Compare impressions with clicks. Open Google Search Console and look at the last 12 months. If impressions stayed steady and clicks fell, people are still seeing you. They're getting the answer without visiting. Check you're looking at the right property first, and compare against the same months last year so seasonality doesn't fool you.
  2. Look at which searches lost clicks. Searches phrased as questions, like how to, is it safe or best for, usually lose clicks first. Searches for your brand name tend to hold up. If your losses sit in the question searches, AI answers are the likely cause.
  3. Ask the AI yourself. Type the five questions your customers ask most into ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google. Note who gets named. If it's your competitor, you've found the real gap.

The first three things I'd fix to show up in AI answers

  1. Put the answers on the page. AI tools can only quote what they can find. If your size guide sits in a PDF and your ingredient list sits inside an image, there's nothing for them to quote. Write the real answers as plain text, close to the product.
  2. Make the page easy for machines to read. Use clear headings, keep one topic per page, and add structured data that says what the product is, what it costs and what people ask about it. That's the reason I'm building my Shopify FAQ generator.
  3. Check you aren't blocking the bots. Some hosting and security tools now block AI crawlers by default. In July 2025, Cloudflare made blocking the default for new domains and started asking every new site at sign-up whether to allow AI crawlers in. It's worth a five-minute check with whoever runs your website.

What I wouldn't do

I wouldn't panic and rewrite the whole site. Some of the traffic you lost was never going to buy. Someone reading what is moringa in an AI answer wasn't about to order a pouch.

I'd also watch sales, leads and sign-ups from search, and not only visits. When I've looked closely, the visits that disappeared first were often the ones that never converted. So before you decide anything, check whether your revenue from search moved, because that tells you whether the drop is actually costing you.

Quick answers

Why are my impressions up but my clicks down?

Usually because people see your result, or an AI answer above it, and get what they need without clicking. Look at which searches lost the clicks before you change anything.

Is it AI Overviews or something else?

Not always AI. Seasonality, a tracking problem, or Google matching you to searches that were never yours can all do it too. That's why step one above compares the same months across years.

How do I get my brand mentioned in ChatGPT and Perplexity?

Start with the three fixes above. Then keep your brand name, product names and category words the same across your store, your Amazon listing and your social profiles, so the tools see one brand and not several. Check the same five questions again each month to see if anything moved.
