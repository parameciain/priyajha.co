Bloom Media Studio · 2022 to 2026

How I ran the agency.

I sold my ownership in May 2026. Bloom still operates.

A margin floor on every account

60% gross margin, checked every month. Accounts under it for two or three months went on review.

Time blocks, not timesheets

Each retainer bought a set number of blocks a month. Allocated Monday, checked Wednesday.

Growth from existing clients

Most new revenue came from adding services for clients we already had. Our biggest retainer grew about 50% that way.

I stopped signing off on everything

A three-step review took my sign-off off the critical path. Ask me why it took me too long.
